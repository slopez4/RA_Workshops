## Learning goals

By the end, RAs will be able to:

1. **Define** nostalgia and describe its typical content, appraisals and functions (regulatory model).
2. **Judge** the strength of the evidence: average effect size, daily-life vs lab, cross-cultural reach.
3. **Place** nostalgia in autobiographical-memory theory (cue effectiveness, encoding specificity, involuntary retrieval, reminiscence bump) and separate *feeling rich* from *being rich*.
4. **Summarize** what is known about smell, music, food, temperature and vision as nostalgia cues, and what is contested (the Proust effect).
5. **Spot** the main confounds in cross-sense comparisons (familiarity, labels, matching, order, sample).
6. **Read** a nostalgia or odor-memory paper with a structured template.
7. **Propose** a testable question with a cue, a control, a measure, a gap statement and a risk.

## Agenda: tight version (≈ 45 min)

- Skip slide 11 (say the two measures aloud on slide 10) and slide 20 (point to it in reference view).

- Keep: slide 2 (tally), slides 13–14 (the Proust effect as a case study in how evidence gets complicated), slide 17 (confounds), slides 21–22 (gaps and seeds), and the pitches.

## Pitch block (10–15 min)

- **Before the session:** each RA has a Module 6 brief from the workbook (even a rough one).
- **Format:** 60 seconds to pitch (question, cue and control, measure, gap and risk), then the group gives **one strength and one concern**. About 2 minutes per RA; for more than 6 RAs, pitch in pairs and report back the best one.
- **Rubric (silent):** Is the question answerable? Is the control appropriate? Does the measure separate feeling from content? Is the gap real (search log)? Is the main risk named?

## Where to cut if time runs short

- Slides 11, 19 and 20 are the easiest to drop in the room; everything on them is also in the reference view.
- **Don't cut** slide 2 (the live tally gives the RAs their own data), slides 13–14 (the Proust effect as an example of critical reading), slide 17 (confounds) or the pitches.

## After the session

- Share the slides link; in reference view (`R`) every slide has a plain-language explanation and the sources.
- RAs finish the workbook (Modules 1–8 as time allows) and bring the brief and cue-diary CSV to the next lab meeting.
- Optional: choose one or two seeds to develop into a small pilot with a faculty or senior-student mentor, and run the literature search properly (Module 4 search log is the starting point).
